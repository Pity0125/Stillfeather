• Drag tabs to reorder them, move them between panes, or drop them outside the window to open a separate window. Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the last closed tab.
• Right-click Favorites to create bookmark groups, then drag folders into a group to pin them. Removing a group keeps its bookmarks.
• Change or disable key bindings in Settings › Shortcuts, with a prompt when bindings conflict. Ctrl+E edits the path and Ctrl+\ toggles dual panes by default.
• Filter folders and search results by date or size. Combine phrases, exclusions, and extensions in a search, such as "annual report" ext:pdf -draft. Results are ranked by relevance.
• Choose from eight themes with light and dark variants. Quick preview settings are simpler, and the path bar and drop-down menus now scale with text size.
• Enter \\nas to browse network shares. New folders immediately enter rename mode, and open tabs update when their folders move or disappear. F1 opens a separate Help window.
