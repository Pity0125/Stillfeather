Detachable tabs, bookmark groups, custom shortcuts, advanced search and eight themes:
• Drag a tab out of the window to open it on its own, or drag tabs to reorder them or move them to the other pane; Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the last closed tab
• Right-click Favorites to create bookmark groups, then drag folders in to pin them
• Change or disable shortcuts in Settings › Shortcuts; dual panes are now Ctrl+\ and editing the path is Ctrl+E
• Filter searches by date and size, or type conditions like ext:pdf and -draft; results are ranked by relevance
• Eight themes, each with light and dark variants, under Settings › Theme & colors
• Type \\nas to browse a NAS's shared folders; F1 opens a separate Help window
• New folders always go straight into rename mode; Shift+Delete always asks before deleting permanently
