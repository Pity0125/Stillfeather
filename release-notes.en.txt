Box selection and empty space, just like File Explorer:
• In Details view, the selection highlight stops at the last column, with space left of the icons and right of the last column
• Press on the empty space beside the items or below the last one and drag to box-select several items; hold Ctrl to toggle, or Shift to add to what's already selected
• Drag past the top or bottom edge to scroll automatically; the box keeps up with the mouse without lag
• Click the empty space to clear the selection, or right-click it for the folder menu; drag files from the items as before
